Our Brotherhood at Clemson

Who We Are

Zeta Psi at Clemson University is a chapter of one of North America's oldest and most distinguished fraternities. We are a tight-knit brotherhood committed to developing well-rounded men of character, intellect, and integrity throughout their college experience and beyond.

Our Values

We are guided by the principles of brotherhood, academic excellence, and service to our community. Our members hold themselves to a high standard in scholarship, leadership on campus, and respect for others — values instilled in every Zeta Psi since 1847.

Our History

Founded at New York University in 1847, Zeta Psi has grown into an international fraternity with chapters across the United States and Canada. Our Clemson chapter continues that rich legacy, producing alumni who lead in business, law, medicine, public service, and the arts.

Life at Clemson

From Homecoming and tailgates to philanthropy drives and formal events, our brothers are active participants in the Clemson community. We partner with the Clemson IFC and campus organizations to make a lasting positive impact on campus and beyond.
