About Zeta Psi International

Our Founding

Zeta Psi was founded on June 1, 1847 at New York University, making it one of the oldest fraternities in North America. The founding brothers established a tradition of academic excellence, brotherhood, and service that continues to define the fraternity today.

Read on Wikipedia

National Headquarters

Zeta Psi International is headquartered and governed by an Executive Director and Board of Trustees made up of alumni and chapter representatives. The national organization supports chapters with resources, programming, and risk management.

Visit zetapsi.org

Chapters Across North America

With over 50 active chapters at leading universities across the United States and Canada, Zeta Psi's brotherhood spans the continent. Our Clemson chapter is part of this larger family of men who share the same values and traditions.

Find Chapters

Annual Convention (Grand Chapter)

Every year, brothers from chapters across North America gather at Grand Chapter — Zeta Psi's national convention. It is an opportunity to vote on fraternity business, compete for awards, and connect with brothers from other schools.

Notable Zeta Psi Alumni

Charles Evans Hughes

Chief Justice of the United States · Class of 1881

Governor of New York, U.S. Secretary of State, and Chief Justice of the Supreme Court. One of the most distinguished statesmen in American history.

William Lyon Mackenzie King

Prime Minister of Canada · Class of 1895

Canada's longest-serving Prime Minister, leading the nation through both World Wars and shaping modern Canadian policy.

Ben Bradlee

Executive Editor, The Washington Post · Class of 1943

Oversaw coverage of the Watergate scandal and the Pentagon Papers, cementing the Post's reputation as a pillar of American journalism.

Kiefer Sutherland

Actor & Producer

Award-winning actor best known for his role as Jack Bauer in the TV series 24, and a prolific career in film and television.

DeWitt Wallace

Co-Founder, Reader's Digest · Class of 1911

Built Reader's Digest into one of the best-selling magazines of the 20th century, with global circulation reaching over 100 million readers.

Alan Thicke

Actor, Songwriter & Television Host

Beloved Canadian entertainer known for Growing Pains, and a prolific songwriter who wrote theme songs for iconic television shows.
