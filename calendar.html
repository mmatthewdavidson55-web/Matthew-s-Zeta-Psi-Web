Subscribe to the Chapter Calendar

To add the official Zeta Psi Clemson calendar to your personal Google Calendar:

  1. Ask your Chapter President or Events Officer for the shared Google Calendar link.
  2. Open Google Calendar and click the "+" icon next to "Other calendars."
  3. Select "Subscribe to calendar" and paste the shared link.
  4. All chapter events will automatically sync to your calendar.

You can also view upcoming events on the Events page at any time.
