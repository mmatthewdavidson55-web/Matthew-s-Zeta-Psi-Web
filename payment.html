Dues & Payments

Chapter dues, fees, and how to pay

Reminder: All dues and fees must be paid by the deadlines set by the Treasurer. Late payments may result in a fine or loss of good-standing status. Contact the Treasurer with any questions or to arrange a payment plan.
💸

Venmo

Send dues and fees directly via Venmo. Include your name and the purpose (e.g., "Fall Dues – [Your Name]") in the note.

@ZetaPsi-Clemson

Update handle with your chapter Treasurer's Venmo

Open Venmo
✉️

Check / Cash

Make checks payable to Zeta Psi – Clemson Chapter and deliver to the Treasurer at any chapter meeting. Cash must be delivered in person.

Please obtain a receipt for all cash payments.

Contact Treasurer
🏦

Zelle / Bank Transfer

Zelle payments can be sent to the chapter's registered email address. Confirm the correct email with the Treasurer before sending.
